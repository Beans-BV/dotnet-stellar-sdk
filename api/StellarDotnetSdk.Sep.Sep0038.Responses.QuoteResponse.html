Remarks

Pass Id as the quote_id of a SEP-6, SEP-24 or SEP-31 transaction to use the quote. The anchor holds the quoted amount in reserve until ExpiresAt.

Every amount and price is read exactly: a value that decimal cannot hold without rounding fails deserialization instead of being approximated.
