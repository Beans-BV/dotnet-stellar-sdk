Server is overloaded; client should retry later.

Remarks

The type-level JsonConverterAttribute is the third and weakest of the three places the strict converter is attached, and it covers the case the other two miss: the enum travelling on its own — a caller's own DTO field, a persisted status column, a queue message — under a JsonSerializerOptions that registers no converter for it. Without it the built-in enum handling maps a bare integer by ordinal, and ordinal 0 here is PENDING, the most optimistic of the four. See EventFilterType for the full three-tier resolution order.
