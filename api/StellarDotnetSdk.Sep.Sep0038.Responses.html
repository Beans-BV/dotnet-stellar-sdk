Namespace StellarDotnetSdk.Sep.Sep0038.Responses

Classes

AssetInfo

An asset the anchor offers in exchange for one or more of the other assets listed by GET /info.

AssetPrice

An indicative price for one asset, as an entry of the buy_assets or sell_assets array of GET /prices.

DeliveryMethod

A method a client can use to deliver funds to the anchor (an entry of sell_delivery_methods) or to receive funds from it (an entry of buy_delivery_methods), as listed by GET /info.

InfoResponse

Response of SEP-38 GET /info: the Stellar and off-chain assets the anchor makes available for trading.

PriceResponse

Response of SEP-38 GET /price: the indicative price for one asset pair.

PricesResponse

Response of SEP-38 GET /prices: indicative prices of the assets available in exchange for the asset given in the request.

QuoteFee

The fee used to calculate a SEP-38 conversion price (the fee object of GET /price, POST /quote and GET /quote/:id). It can be used to show the price components to the user.

QuoteFeeDetail

One component of a QuoteFee, such as a service fee or a payment-rail fee.

QuoteResponse

A firm SEP-38 quote, as returned by POST /quote and GET /quote/:id.
