Remarks

With fee in the sell asset, sell_amount - fee = price * buy_amount; with it in the buy asset, sell_amount = price * (buy_amount + fee). Whatever the fee asset, sell_amount = total_price * buy_amount.

The specification does not require Asset to be the sell or the buy asset, so branch on it before applying either fee formula. The client validates neither the fee's asset nor the sign of any fee amount.
