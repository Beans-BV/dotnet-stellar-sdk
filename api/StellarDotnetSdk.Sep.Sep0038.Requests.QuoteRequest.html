Remarks

Provide exactly one of SellAmount or BuyAmount, and at most one of SellDeliveryMethod or BuyDeliveryMethod. Both rules are checked before the request is sent.

Unless GET /info lists no delivery methods (or only one) for the off-chain asset, the anchor also requires the delivery method for that asset. That depends on the anchor's GET /info response, so it is left to the anchor to enforce.

ExpireAfter

Optional: the desired expiry of the quote. The anchor may choose a later expires_at, and should answer 400 Bad Request if it cannot offer an expiry on or after this time. Sent as UTC, rounded up to the whole second so that an anchor storing whole seconds can honor it exactly; a quote expiring before the sent time is rejected.
