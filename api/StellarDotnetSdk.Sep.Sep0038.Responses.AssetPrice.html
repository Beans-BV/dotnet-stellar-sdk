Remarks

In BuyAssets (a request with sell_asset), it is the price of one unit of Asset in terms of the requested sell asset: selling BRL for USDC at 5.42 means one USDC costs 5.42 BRL, as in the specification's sell-side example.

In SellAssets (a request with buy_asset), it is the price of one unit of the requested buy asset in terms of Asset: buying USDC with BRL at 5.42 means one USDC costs 5.42 BRL, as in the specification's buy-side example.

Read exactly: a value that decimal cannot hold without rounding fails deserialization instead of being approximated.
